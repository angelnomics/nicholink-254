ABOUT NICHO LINK 254

About NichoLink 254

NichoLink 254 is a rental property platform designed to make it easier for tenants to discover available homes and connect with landlords.

The platform brings property listings, property information, viewing requests, messaging and other rental tools together in one place.

Our goal

Our goal is to make finding and managing rental-property interactions simpler, clearer and more organized for tenants and landlords.

How it works

  • Tenants browse available properties.
  • Tenants can save properties they are interested in.
  • Tenants can contact landlords and request property viewings.
  • Landlords can manage their properties and respond to viewing requests.
  • Users can receive messages and notifications about platform activity.

SUPPORT

Help Center

How do I find a property?

Open the Properties page and use the available location, bedroom, price and sorting options.

How do I save a property?

Click the ❤️ Save Property button on a property card or on the property-details page. Saved properties appear in your tenant dashboard.

How do I contact a landlord?

Open a property and select Message Landlord. You can also use the available WhatsApp contact option.

How do I request a viewing?

Open the property's details page, complete the viewing-request form and submit it.

Where can I see my notifications?

Open the Notifications section from your dashboard to view messages, viewing updates and other account activity.

How do I report a suspicious listing?

Use the Report a Listing section below and provide as much accurate information as possible.

SAFETY

Safety Tips

Before paying

  • Verify the property and landlord before making significant payments.
  • Be cautious if someone pressures you to pay immediately.
  • Be especially careful with requests to send money to an unrelated person's account.

When arranging a viewing

  • Confirm the property details before travelling to a viewing.
  • Consider meeting in an appropriate, safe environment.
  • Tell someone you trust where you are going for a viewing.

Protect your personal information

  • Never share your password with another person.
  • Be careful with requests for passwords, verification codes or sensitive information.
  • Use strong, unique login credentials.

Suspicious activity

Stop communicating and report the listing if something feels suspicious, misleading or unsafe.

NichoLink 254 is a platform for connecting users. Always use your own judgment and verify important information before making payments or entering into a rental agreement.
